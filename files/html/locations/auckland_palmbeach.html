Palm Beach

Auckland Tourist Location

A Beautiful Beach on Waiheke Island!

Palm Beach is a beautiful sandy beach located on Waiheke Island, just a short ferry trip from Auckland. It is known for its clear water, golden sand, and peaceful surroundings, making it a popular destination for visitors looking to relax and enjoy Auckland's natural scenery.

Visitors can spend the day swimming, sunbathing, walking along the beach, or exploring the surrounding area. The beach is also a great location for photography, with scenic views of the coastline and surrounding landscape. Palm Beach is generally quieter than some of Auckland's busier beaches, making it a good option for visitors wanting a more relaxing experience.

Palm Beach is free to visit, but visitors should consider the cost of travelling to Waiheke Island. Ferry tickets, food, and other activities can increase the overall cost of a visit. Despite these expenses, Palm Beach can be an affordable destination for visitors who simply want to enjoy the beach and scenery.

Facts:

  • Located on Waiheke Island
  • Short ferry trip from Auckland
  • Beautiful sandy beach
  • Popular for swimming and relaxing
  • Great location for photography
  • Surrounded by scenic coastal views
  • Free to access

Costs:

  • Entry to Palm Beach: Free
  • Ferry from Auckland: approximately $20-$50 return
  • Estimated food costs: $10-$30 per person
  • Estimated local transport: $5-$20
  • Estimated total visit cost: $35-$100 per person
