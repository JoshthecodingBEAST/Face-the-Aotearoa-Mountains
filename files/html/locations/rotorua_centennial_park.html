Centennial Park

Rotorua Tourist Location

The Cherry blossoms of Rotorua

Centennial Park is a peaceful public park located in Rotorua, known for its beautiful gardens, open spaces, and seasonal cherry blossoms. The park is a great place to relax, take photographs, and enjoy the natural scenery away from the busier parts of the city. During spring, the cherry blossom trees provide a colourful display that makes the park especially popular with visitors.

Visitors can walk around the park, enjoy the gardens, have a picnic, or simply relax and take in the surrounding scenery. The park is a good choice for visitors looking for a free and relaxing activity while exploring Rotorua. Its peaceful atmosphere also makes it a suitable location for families and people interested in photography and nature.

Facts:

  • Located in Rotorua
  • Known for its seasonal cherry blossoms
  • Free public park
  • Great location for photography
  • Suitable for walking and relaxing
  • Open outdoor spaces and gardens
  • A peaceful activity for visitors to Rotorua
