Wellingtons Oriental Bay!

Wellington Tourist Location

Wellington's Beautiful Waterfront!

Oriental Bay is one of Wellington's most popular beaches and is located just a short distance from the city centre. The beach is surrounded by beautiful waterfront scenery and provides views across Wellington Harbour, making it a popular place for both locals and visitors.

Visitors can relax on the sandy beach, swim in the harbour, walk along the waterfront, or enjoy the surrounding parks and cafes. Oriental Bay is also a great location for photography, particularly around sunrise and sunset when the harbour and surrounding hills create scenic views.

The beach is easily accessible from central Wellington by walking, cycling, driving, or public transport. Since there is no admission fee, Oriental Bay is a good option for visitors looking for a relaxing and affordable activity while exploring Wellington.

Facts:

  • Located close to Wellington city centre
  • Popular sandy beach
  • Overlooks Wellington Harbour
  • Popular for swimming and relaxing
  • Walking and cycling paths nearby
  • Surrounded by cafes and restaurants
  • Great location for photography

Costs:

  • Entry to Oriental Bay: Free
  • Swimming and beach access: Free
  • Estimated food costs: $10-$30 per person
  • Estimated public transport costs: $2-$10
  • Estimated parking costs: $0-$10
  • Estimated total visit cost: $12-$50 per person
